Why Subscribe

Because flour, like fruit, is fresher when it has not waited.

The thing about stone-milled flour is that it is alive. The oils oxidise, the aroma fades, the nutrition slowly slips. The kindest thing you can do for your atta is to use it within a few weeks of milling.

Subscribing means we mill on the morning of dispatch, instead of waiting for an order to drop. You get fresher flour, we mill in a steady rhythm, our farmers receive predictable orders. Everyone wins, including the grain.

"I have not bought atta from the supermarket in fourteen months. My rotis are softer, my mother is impressed." Reema S. · Bengaluru, subscriber since 2024
